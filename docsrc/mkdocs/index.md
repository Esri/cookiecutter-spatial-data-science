# Cookiecutter-Spatial-Data-Science 3.2.0-dev0

This template integrates ArcGIS Pro projects and tools with data science workflows. Please
think of this template as a collection of best practices, and little more. Use what works. 
Remove what does not. If you decide to change something, please just do it consistently in 
your project. This way when you look at it in six months, *you* can still understand what 
you were doing, and so can *other* people.
